what is banchan?

Small dishes, 정 big feelings

In Korea, no meal is served alone. Little plates of banchan — kimchi, seasoned greens, sweet roots — crowd around your rice like old friends. We make them fresh each week, so your busiest days still taste like someone cares. That feeling has a name: 정 (jeong).
