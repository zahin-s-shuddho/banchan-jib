what is banchan?

Little plates around your rice

In Korea no meal is served alone. Small dishes of banchan (kimchi, seasoned greens, sweet roots) sit around the rice. We make them at home, in small batches, so a busy day still ends with a proper meal.

That caring feeling has a name, 정 (jeong). We try to put it in every cup.
