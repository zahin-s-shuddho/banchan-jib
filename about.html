The grandmother next door

Banchan Jib isn't a restaurant. It's a feeling — the one you get when someone quietly leaves food at your door because they know you've had a long day. We call her 할머니, Halmeoni — meet her below, or find her wandering the site.

손으로
만든
MADE BY HAND

If Banchan Jib were a person, she would be a warm, kind-hearted grandmother living next door to someone with a busy life.

Knowing they barely have time to prepare a proper meal, she quietly makes a selection of flavourful Korean side dishes each week and leaves them at their doorstep — insisting she simply made too much.

She is not loud, trendy, or attention-seeking. She is calm, observant, and thoughtful. She notices more than she speaks, and expresses care through small, consistent acts rather than grand gestures.

Her side dishes feel like a warm hug — the kind that brings tears to your eyes on rough days when you've been bottling everything up.
